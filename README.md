## Hello, I'm Teetouch 👋

AI engineer working on Thai document AI: OCR, vision-language models and benchmarks.
Computer Engineering student at King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL).
Building [Remtir](https://github.com/Remtir), a Thai AI research lab for computer vision ([AksonOCR-Lite](https://huggingface.co/Remtir/AksonOCR-Lite)).

## Publications

- **Typhoon OCR: Open Vision-Language Model For Thai Document Extraction**. An open VLM for Thai/English document extraction, fine-tuned with a multi-stage data pipeline that combines traditional OCR and synthetic data. [Technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14722)
- **ThaiOCRBench: A Task-Diverse Benchmark for Vision-Language Understanding in Thai**. The first comprehensive benchmark for evaluating VLMs on Thai text-rich visual understanding. [IJCNLP-AACL 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04479)
- **Typhoon 2: A Family of Open Text and Multimodal Thai Large Language Models**. Text and multimodal LLMs optimized for Thai. [Technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13702)
- **ThaiTC: Thai Transformer-based Image Captioning**. End-to-end transformer image captioning for Thai. [iSAI-NLP 2022](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9960246)
- **Eye Landmarks Detection Using RT-DETR With Rules**. Detects nine eye landmarks with RT-DETR at 0.974 accuracy despite partial face visibility. [ECTI-CON 2023](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Eye-Landmarks-Detection-using-RT-DETR-with-Rules-Boonnithititikul-Jaknamon/355ccc51a53f83f582eb56aba8e97aacf0f688e3)
